# The Moon in a Jar

I found the moon on the kitchen shelf,
wedged in a jar with the jam.
It hummed like a fridge when the house is asleep,
and it was not as small as I am.

I loosened the lid, as my grandmother said —
*anything worth keeping should breathe* —
and the kitchen went silver, the kettle, the bread,
and the garden, and everything, and me.
