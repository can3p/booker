# The Jar {#jar}

The jar had been on the shelf for as long as Mia could remember. It was green, a little cloudy, and it hummed -- very quietly -- whenever the moon was full. "It's only the wind," her grandmother said, but there was no wind in the kitchen, and Mia knew it.

She waited until the house was asleep. Then she climbed onto the chair, and the chair onto the counter, and she lifted the jar down with both hands. It was warmer than she expected --- warm like a cup of cocoa...

Inside, something moved.

---

In the morning the jar was back on its shelf. Three things were different:

- the porridge was *silver*
- the cat would not come into the kitchen
- her grandmother was **humming**

> "Anything worth keeping," her grandmother said, "needs to breathe."
